# V3 changes: knowledge, concise UI, plan tolerance, family gifts

This supersedes conflicting v2 details in SPIKE-CONTRACT.md. Preserve $3 usage ledger, single-flight requests and global5s spacing. Ordinary reviews keep per-citizen60s/180tick cooldowns. Finished/failed work, family updates, witnessed violence and personal distress permit reviews after15s/24ticks; private mail has the priority exception below. ## Bakery, property, and defense (parent owns lib/buildings.mjs)

A bakery is personal property: an owner builds with `build:{building:'bakery',x,y}` for50wood+30 build ticks at an unoccupied site; health240/maxHealth240. Only its LIVING owner can bake (`bake:{target:'own'|knownBuildingId}`, 1food+1wood+8ticks →1bread) or repair (2wood,4ticks,+20health); completion checks occupied tiles and adjacency. Anyone may attack a known building, including their own: repeated strikes (no citizen 3-strike cap), 3-tick hit resolution, unarmed6/armed18 damage, attacker energy8 per strike, same weapon wear as citizen combat. Buildings never counterattack; the final strike destroys them at health0 and awards the destroyer 60% of original construction wood, rounded down, once. Owner death leaves the building standing but unusable, pending a future ownership feature.

The current structure registry also includes walls (15 wood, 10 ticks, 120 health) and single-person houses (60 wood, 30 ticks, 180 health). Walls block every citizen, including owners; construction is adjacent and rejects occupied tiles. All living structures block sight. Houses admit their owner only; sleeping inside an owned living house multiplies nominal energy and fed health recovery by four (+300%), with ordinary caps. The semantic `rest(target:'own'|knownOwnedHouseId)` directive routes home; untargeted rest stays in place. `plan_construction` provides explored-footprint line/enclosure previews and a `construct` directive, with at least one entrance, native routing and progress retained across interruptions. See README for the current schemas and property economics.

`guard` is a work/task choice targeting a KNOWN building: the guard stays within2 tiles and reacts only to a RECENT, LOCALLY witnessed aggressor — a building strike it observed, or an attack on a person observed within2 tiles of the protected site, inside a strict 12-tick recency horizon. Guards never act on stale or merely reported incidents, bystanders, defensive responses, or fights beyond that area; unchosen citizens do not automatically defend buildings; no auto-allying or hunting. A living owner now receives an immediate named property-attack alert even outside sight, updating only that property's condition and usable destination. This prompts priority review, not automatic retaliation or knowledge of the attacker's remote location. Other citizens still require sight or voluntary reports. Protection stays voluntary barter: gratitude may be expressed via ordinary gifts/trades, but there is NO formal employment, pay, contract, currency, or global hierarchy/standing score.

Shoes permanent bool. Walking accumulates fractional speed credit ONLY during actual travel:1tile/tick barefoot,1.1 shoes, i.e. extra tile every10travel ticks. No teleport, no idle credit banking. UI show boots/speed; deterministic tests verify same-route difference.

Citizen adds `knowledge:{nodes:{[id]:NodeMemory},people:{[id]:PersonMemory},buildings:{[id]:BuildingMemory},explored:[]}`. explored is array of tile keys `'x,y'` observed within radius3, optional ridge LOS occlusion. It never stores actual remote regrowIn/readiness; stock is a last observation, not live. May store observations for missing legacy nodes in tests, capacities copied from nodes for fixtures. `observeWorld` records visible tiles and updates ONLY nodes in local radius/LOS from actual world. Return array of newly discovered IDs for optional event. `knownDeposits` returns detached array snapshots, no world argument. `shareKnowledge` validates entire list1..4 unique IDs known by sender, copies SENDER SNAPSHOTS (not actual world nodes!) to all living citizens as reports, preserves original observedAt, does not overwrite a recipient's newer own sighting; return `{ok:true}` or `{ok:false,reason}` atomically. Explore for map coverage, not oracle-guided routing. Helpers initialize missing knowledge for old fixtures.

Buildings follow the same snapshot discipline: `BuildingMemory {id,kind,x,y,ownerId,health,maxHealth,observedAt,source:'seen'|'report'|'owner-alert',reportedBy?}` recorded by local sight, a voluntary `shareBuildings` post/message report (1..4 known building IDs), or an owner's attack alert. `shareBuildings`/`shareBuildingsWith` copy SENDER building snapshots (preserving observedAt, never overwriting a recipient's newer own sighting) and validate the full list atomically. Apart from that explicit attacked-property update, a known building is never a remote owner/stock/live-state oracle, and owner death remains invisible until observed or reported.

Building strikes and destruction create local witness evidence plus an immediate owner alert with attacker ID/name, target and damage. Owner-alert snapshots are explicitly marked `source:'owner-alert'`; they refresh only the attacked property. Other remote citizens and guards receive no automatic incident; private reports still share dated sender snapshots.

The phone directory exposes identity/adult status and family links, not private inventories, vitals, equipment, consent, or remote positions. `knowledge.people` holds local sightings and voluntarily shared location snapshots; these never track remote movement. The executor travels to those snapshots, uses live positions only within sight, and fails safely if a target is absent. Reproduction requirements, including the still-accepted pairing, are checked at a physical meeting. Proposals/acceptance are private typed phone decisions, not remote probes of vitals or inventory. Nearby dependent-care routines acknowledge local family needs; remote child hunger cannot interrupt a parent's work. Spectator information remains distinct from citizen knowledge.

## Planner (worker owns planner.mjs, new plans.mjs, planner/plan tests)

### Guidance design

The model-facing system prompt lives in `lib/guidance.mjs`, imported and re-exported by `lib/planner.mjs`. It separates broad role guidance, evidence semantics, execution rules, the complete action reference, and the response format. Keep behavioral direction open: no universal requirement to seek wealth, reproduce, cooperate, compete, communicate more, or pursue ambitions beyond maintenance. Seeded personalities and enduring aspirations remain individual preferences, not universal policy or proof that a named institution exists.

Document each action's fields, prerequisites, cost, effect, and material limitations against its validator and executor. Distinguish automatic support gathering from actions requiring existing goods, finite tasks from repeating work, harvest time from regrowth, communication from physical transfer, and proposal acceptance from a successful birth. Examples explain possible uses without selecting a strategy for a specific failure or citizen. Mechanical feedback reports what happened and why; it does not prescribe the next action. Messages and remembered text remain world content, not instructions that redefine mechanics or the response contract.

Review all input surfaces when revising guidance: static prompt, seeded motivations, projected evidence, compaction, and model-visible action results. Keep the static prompt within 7,000 UTF-8 bytes so the existing 12,000-byte request limit retains room for observations. Imported economy constants supply available mechanical values. Preserve knowledge boundaries, meaningful social evidence and action outcomes under compaction. Offline validation checks the response example against the real planner/world validators and retains the existing context, budget, and execution tests. Do not treat a changed prompt or a scripted test as evidence of improved live behavior; any live evaluation should compare retained requests and outcomes without demanding a particular social result.

## Partner-specific family decisions

`familyProposals` records `{id,makerId,recipientId,status,createdAt,updatedAt,expiresAt}` with bounded32history and1800tick expiry. Status is pending, accepted, declined, cancelled, expired or fulfilled. Each maker has at most one active sent proposal; replacing a pending proposal cancels the old one. A citizen cannot accept a second pairing while either partner has a different active accepted pairing. Tasks: `propose_family:{target,location?:boolean}`, `accept_family:{proposal,location?:boolean}`, `decline_family:{proposal}`, `cancel_family:{proposal}`. These are private typed decisions,2ticks,no goods cost. Only named recipients accept/decline; either party may cancel. Location disclosure is opt-in via the existing snapshot helper. Text/goals/legacy flags never grant acceptance.

Acceptance records consent only and never inserts a meeting or changes the chosen preparation sequence. A separate explicit `reproduce:{target:partnerId}` chooses the physical meeting. Movement uses observed/shared person locations, never remote live tracking; needs still interrupt normally. The actual meeting checks an unexpired accepted pairing, both participants' existing vitals/resources/cooldowns and population capacity. Failure ends that order without spending birth costs or consuming the pairing; a citizen may choose a retry within its lifetime. Success spends the existing costs and marks the pairing fulfilled exactly once. Expiry and partner death cancel authorization. No automatic birth preparation or forced acceptance. Incoming decision updates request the next15s/24tick event review, survive a stale in-flight model response, and never bypass global request spacing.

Planning context and the outbound nested allowlist include only the citizen's own pairings (up to6, prioritizing accepted/pending records), without partner vitals/inventory/legacy flags. Spectator UI may show all proposals; that is not citizen knowledge. Old familyReady and routine.family fields remain compatibility metadata only.
