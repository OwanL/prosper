## Working design and acceptance

1. **Body:** synchronous authoritative game actions and accounting; pathfinding, reservations, combat, consent and settlement stay deterministic.
2. **Local mind:** utility/rule policies execute continuously with observed evidence, trait variation, relationships, resource disputes, survival, trading and family preparation. This works with no model and while a model is unavailable or thinking.
3. **Strategic mind:** one compact structured policy revision, normally one request, with bounded output and infrequent staggered admission. Public/known information only. Messages and mundane outcomes are folded into bounded evidence rather than prompting immediate paid conversations.
4. **Presentation:** visible territorial claims/alliances, attack and speech cues and consequential story beats. Model reasoning is a separate indicator from physical work.

Policy `noGo` rectangles are hard travel and work exclusions, including food trips. A citizen already inside a newly forbidden area may take a safe outward route, but cannot re-enter. If the boundary blocks known food, the local controller records salient survival evidence and requests a policy review; it does not silently cross the boundary or substitute a rescue action. Territory remains an announced contested demand, not a travel restriction or automatic ownership.

Acceptance requires normal seeded trials and 100+ citizen throughput/survival evidence, not only scripted conflict fixtures. Measure actual requests/tokens per citizen-hour when live samples justify it. Do not describe deterministic behavior as spontaneous model emergence. Preserve restore compatibility and prove conservation/cancellation under asynchronous responses.

## Integrated implementation

The current default is a continuous local mind plus optional `set_policy`, prompt `prosper-policy-4`. New runtime worlds enable autonomy; restored worlds preserve their existing citizens. Local AI opens no model turns. Live defaults to already-installed local Qwen 3.5 9B; exact cloud aliases require explicit configuration. A single strict response can revise rules/goals/memory/messages, while ordinary movement, food, production, markets and negotiations continue asynchronously.

Compact live tools cannot invoke hidden primitive/query/debug tools. Partial policy fields persist, and explicit null clears home/territory/partner. A newer explicit policy revision supersedes an older inference without retrying. Ordinary autonomous changes remain valid. Known-person/map checks, hard no-go travel/work exclusions, revocable consent, actual delivered negotiations and atomic settlement remain authoritative. The independent review found and fixed obsolete courtship, rival-family acceptance, unclear commitment withdrawal, active no-go routes and unbounded retained state; see [review](overnight-review.md).

Explicitly naming an existing pact member as a rival publicly withdraws the actor from that conflicting pact, preserving everyone else's membership. Pending offers are revoked; a cooldown and rival-membership check prevent immediate indirect rejoining. This gives strategic policy a predictable way to renounce a faction without expelling other people.

Reviews are opportunities, not mandatory paid upkeep. Thriving citizens skip due dates; repeated fresh harm, substantial resource disputes, genuine local stalls, failed critical food search, blocked no-go survival or an infrequent 10,000-tick age review can qualify. The last attempted review persists for failure hysteresis. Normal output cap is 1,200, concurrency one, one request per review, with at least 120 simulation ticks and 30 wall seconds between sends plus a 30/hour ceiling. Locked durable reservations enforce wall/hour guards after reload. Finite lifetime budgets never increase automatically. Damaged accounting closes inference without stopping physics.

Spectator transport omits notebooks before cloning, while checkpoints and the selected observation endpoint preserve them. A separate consequential ring (256 total, at most 64 per kind) prevents mundane work and frequent trades erasing rarer violence/family stories. Personal observers still require an actual event audience; local witnesses are recorded at occurrence. Camera/event markers are presentation, never inferred action outcomes. A small New citizens control sets 1–250 founders for Load/Reset; defaults remain immediately usable in Local AI.

## Reproduction and limitations

Exact normal run, 100-citizen API, model setup, reset/persistence and verification commands are in the [README](../../README.md). Offline policy probes are deliberately gated; real runtime checks use `npm.cmd exec -- tsx scripts/probe-runtime.ts` after build. `npm.cmd exec -- tsx scripts/benchmark-harness.ts` measures whole-request sizes and eligibility with mock continuation policies, never actual model quality/tokens.

Territorial extortion, local reprisals, ally defense, gifts, negotiated families and inherited/mutated traits are implemented. **Secret blackmail is not**: there is no compromising-information/disclosure mechanic. Advanced coalition strategy, logistics/blockades and sophisticated long-term goal inheritance remain incomplete. The remote global market intentionally leaves extraction and owned stations as the main geographic leverage. Maintenance adds modest wood/fibre demand, not a verified complete late-game economy. Saturation checks reduce runaway useless hoarding; they do not solve every demand incentive. Bounded memory may forget old history, and symbolic summaries are partial by design. Local Qwen's validated replies can still be poor policies; no claim of equivalent cheap-model intelligence is made.
