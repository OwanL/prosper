## Summary and confidence

This live run produced **four births, one starvation death, nine living citizens, ten completed stations, 22 matched market fills and no recorded attack attempts**. Five surviving founders and one offspring have all T3 tools; another offspring has a full T2 kit. Relationships have consequential boundaries and refusals, and children develop their own productive and social choices. That is evidence of this run's behavior, not proof of general emergence, personality causality, or success across seeds.

The world is **exactly the same tick5573 world as the previous audit**, not a newly advanced run. A new read-only capture and deep comparison confirmed this. The user's statement that it ran for a while is consistent with the earlier advance from approximately930 to5573; no further progression was present when this investigation started.

### Strongest positive evidence

- Two voluntary pairings produced two actual children each, with reciprocal consent and per-parent costs. Refusal did not mechanically imply consent.
- Cora maintained independence, while family-oriented founders pursued parenthood; Bram sought leverage and Dara emphasized pride and territory. Citizens did not all choose the same livelihood or relationship path.
- The oldest offspring,1881, built a T3 mill, traded and courted;2751 built two stations and assisted a sibling; younger children bootstrapped more slowly.
- Market settlement and final currency conservation reconcile exactly. Poor cash balances do not necessarily mean poor material holdings.
- Six stale craft/build repetitions were rejected before strategy metadata committed. The guard prevented real duplicate discrete work without rewriting intentions.

### Strongest negative evidence

- **Bram died at2944 with155 gold.** He confused hunger onset with imminent death, trusted false food-delivery claims, kept producing wood, and committed self-rescue only16 ticks before death.
- **Physical reproduction and strategy feedback disagreed.** A live joint action was described as displaced; replacement cancelled it. After a successful retry/birth, Eli's strategy still reported cancellation and held up subsequent work.
- **Acceptance repeatedly became invented completion.** Citizens claimed filled orders, equipped tools, collected loot and operating stations without the corresponding receipts. Some false claims persisted in memories or influenced other citizens.
- **7782 is nearly hungry at the cutoff:** fullness2.09, no food. A food-first journey is already active and may succeed before starvation, but it is not a completed rescue.
- There was **no violence**, despite scarcity, territorial claims and a rich citizen dying. Most disputes became boundaries, price agreements or peaceful refusal.
- “No gifts/no debt” became a pervasive rule of social conduct, even though gifts are mechanically permitted and the personalities do not require refusing family help. This is observed convergence, not an established causal diagnosis of the prompt.

### Important distinctions

**World facts** outrank receipts of mere acceptance; **completion receipts** outrank citizen claims; **current private state** outranks a memory of an earlier state. Citizen-authored notes are beliefs, not authoritative history. The report distinguishes observed defects, source-supported mechanisms, interpretations and untested remedies.

### Reading the citations

Unless described as application/completion, a turn tick is usually its **observation/request-start tick**. A response may commit dozens or hundreds of ticks later. Several apparent contradictions become understandable only when those times are separated; delayed context does not excuse claiming an outcome that its own receipt rejected.

A frozen copy, derived data and agent notes were kept under OS temp, outside durable simulation data. The findings needed for future work are included in this document; the canonical ledger remains the evidence source after temporary files disappear.

## Implementation follow-up (2026-10-07)

The investigation above describes the pre-fix implementation and remains historical evidence. Source line references may move as fixes land. The [follow-up learnings](../LEARNINGS.md#investigation-follow-up-fixes-2026-10-07) record the changes:

- **Joint-action lifecycle fixed:** core-consented shared reproduction binds both matching directives to the actual action ID. A real sequential-tick regression also covers an old cancellation consumed before the new shared action starts. Only the exact stale joint-cancellation block rejoins; unrelated or withdrawn work is not revived. Both follow-on directives advance, with one birth and one cost/refund per parent.
- **Receipt evidence clarified:** order responses now expose actual filled/remaining quantities and gold paid/received, including mixed resting prices. Acceptance is not represented as future delivery. Messages/memories are still citizen-authored; false claims are not automatically censored or repaired.
- **Decision context/guidance revised to version15:** hunger onset is separate from current-health starvation/death horizon; inference age and reservations are explicit. Append is additional output. Tool tiers, metadata bounds, dependent claims, live joint actions and voluntary gifts are clarified.

These changes were checked with389 passing tests and a successful build/typecheck, not a paid live evaluation. The same run remained paused at5573 with a deeply equal world and unchanged spending. Existing choices, notes, family relationships and already committed work were not rewritten. Better survival, reporting, family help or conflict is not yet demonstrated; provider delays and occasional empty responses remain unresolved.
