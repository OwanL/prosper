# Overnight implementation review, 8 October 2026

Independent mechanical review of the overnight redesign. The repository was already dirty at review start; this review changed only focused regression tests and this note. It did not call a live provider, start/stop services, or write to either durable Prosper data directory.

## Confirmed defects and fixes

- **Rival policy did not govern family offers.** A citizen with `rivals: [B]` could accept B's incoming family offer or select B as its default partner. The world owner now excludes rivals from unsolicited acceptance and default selection, while an explicit `partnerId` remains an intentional override. Regression: `test/overnight-review-rivals.test.ts`.
- **A new policy could leave obsolete courtship active.** After a family offer created pending consent, revising the partner to a rival did not revoke consent or clear the pending meeting. `setAutonomyPolicy` now revokes consent and removes the pending family state. The same path handles reproduction avoidance and explicit partner changes. Regression: `test/overnight-review-rivals.test.ts`.
- **Explicit policy choices could not be cleared.** Strict policy patches had no representation for abandoning a partner, home, or territory. Nullable patch fields now clear those values; territory clearing also removes the active claim and suppresses immediate recreation. Regression checks schema acceptance, consent cleanup, and claim removal.
- **An old asynchronous response could replace a newer explicit decision.** A delayed strategic response could commit after a newer policy revision. The runner now snapshots `policyRevision` and rejects only superseded explicit policy responses; ordinary local territory/action changes during inference remain valid. Existing strategic-harness coverage checks this distinction.
- **Checkpoint validation rejected newly added autonomy fields.** The strict schema omitted `claimSuppressed`; restored autonomous citizens with that field failed validation. The schema now accepts it. Review regressions also round-trip policy revision, claims, social inbox state, and the persisted last strategic-attempt tick.
- **No-go policy was not safe to revise while active.** A citizen already inside a newly forbidden rectangle could not route out, and an in-flight route/work site could continue under stale path permissions. Pathfinding now permits an outward escape through initially enclosing rectangles and forbids re-entry. Policy changes cancel only affected actions and return reserved inputs. Blocked known food is recorded as survival evidence and makes strategic review eligible without crossing the boundary. Tests cover escape, route cancellation, unrelated-action preservation, craft-input refund, and blocked hunger.
- **Retained social/exploration state needed explicit bounds.** Cleanup now caps long-lived visited tiles, relationships, threats, permits, and sent signals, with TTL pruning. A cadence-phase regression injects oversized maps and checks bounded cleanup with room for the cleanup interval.
- **Intentional surplus waits looked like failed work.** The final evidence review found that a citizen deliberately pausing saturated extraction could satisfy the generic 600-tick no-progress condition. The controller now records an explicit checkpointed `idleReason:'surplus'` outcome; generic stall admission excludes it. New harm, failed survival and the 10,000-tick age review still qualify, and new local work or an explicit policy revision clears the marker. The focused regression distinguishes a deliberate pause from genuinely stalled work and checks age review; checkpoint round-trip covers the marker.

## Review evidence

The review checked the autonomous policy and economy paths, escrow accounting, observation projection, checkpoint schema, compact runner admission and stale-response handling, and the spectator's selected-knowledge scene. Market sell inventory is moved into `escrowItems`, and reserved action inputs leave carried inventory; the proposed food-runway/escrow concern was not a defect. UI knowledge projection receives observation entities and public geometry, omits global claims/alliances and spectator-only state, and suppresses the world pulse and story rail in knowledge view. Public claims and alliances are available to strategic reasoning through the explicit observation contract.

The runner stamps `Citizen.review.strategicTick` before a compact attempt, so failed calls participate in hysteresis; a successful policy commit also advances `autonomy.reviewedTick`. Routine messages/trades do not wake the strategic model. Repeated fresh harm, qualifying resource disputes, local stalls, blocked no-go survival, or the infrequent 10,000-tick age review can qualify when the citizen's persisted due tick arrives. The rolling request ledger, not scheduler eligibility, remains the spend authority.
