## Objective

Citizens choose consequential priorities and revise them from evidence. The simulation carries out routine navigation, harvesting, needs and care. Neither a communication quota nor a prescribed business, family or combat outcome defines success. Evaluate completed intentions, adaptation, exchange, competition and survival alongside latency, failures and cost.

## Evidence behind the change

The high-reasoning trial is preserved in `.prosper/experiments/high-reasoning-2026-10-06`. It produced three bakeries and a birth. Returned reasoning metadata confirms that reasoning ran. This is an uncontrolled trajectory: earlier low-effort plans had already accumulated construction materials, and the output ceiling also changed. It does not establish a causal improvement from effort alone.

High reasoning still queued actions with conflicting costs. Lio's phone send consumed wood needed by reproduction. Calls took roughly 4–12 seconds at 8× simulation speed, allowing dozens of ticks of change before a decision arrived. Large snapshots, raw coordinates, serial plans and wholesale replacement make reasoning needlessly difficult.

## Design

- Semantic places: four stable areas partition the small map. Labels describe identity, not invented biomes. Resource summaries count only personally observed or explicitly reported sites and disclose their age. Routing uses simulation geometry; models receive travel estimates and place IDs.
- Progressive information: a small initial observation contains own state, current execution, immediate correspondence and available tool names. Queries return bounded facts about resources, people, buildings, exchanges, family, history or mechanics. Unseen inventories and remote live stock stay private.
- Typed tools: query tools are separate from decisions. Phone and escrow tools return real receipts, allowing subsequent calls to refer to actual offer IDs. Physical actions become validated durable directives, with semantic destinations resolved by the simulation.
- Execution continuity: ordinary reasoning runs while physical work continues. Continuing a directive preserves effort. Replacing it is explicit. Failures and materially new messages require review; primitive progress is not a reason to call the model.
- Neutral agency: personality and evidence govern cooperation, bargaining, withholding, defense or aggression. Capability descriptions specify costs and consequences without mandating social behavior.
- Bounded loops: limit calls, tool results and elapsed reasoning per turn; reserve and settle every provider call. Log receipts, directive acceptance, stale decisions and resource costs. A rejected argument returns useful feedback within the turn rather than stopping the world.

Pie reference principles come from `harness/tools/README.md`, `harness/tools/result-processing/README.md` and `harness/tool-and-skill-selection/README.md`: explicit capability ownership, injected runtime ports, bounded results with disclosure of omissions, and recoverable capability discovery. Prosper does not import Pie runtime dependencies or credentials.

### Moving contacts and action preparation

The user identified spatial coordination as a likely obstruction and refined the desired solution to visible pursuit and queued movement. Three approaches were considered: area-wide interaction, dynamic citizen destinations with local search, or explicit appointment/rendezvous contracts. Dynamic destinations reuse existing actions and preserve meaningful physical proximity with the least new world machinery. Area-wide interaction and appointment contracts were not implemented.

Previously, native `move(target: citizenId)` compiled a remembered person into fixed coordinates. It now retains the citizen ID internally as an `approach` directive; observations and receipts consistently present it as `move`. While the citizen is visible, each movement step routes toward the current sighting. Without sight, code searches an explicit area hint, the last personally observed/reported area, or the caller's current area.
